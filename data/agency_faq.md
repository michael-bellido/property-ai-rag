# Sunset Realty Group — Frequently Asked Questions

## Viewings & Booking
Viewings can be scheduled Monday to Saturday, 9:00 to 19:00 local time. We recommend booking at least 24 hours in advance. Virtual viewings via video call are available for international buyers who cannot visit in person before making an offer.

## Buying Process for Non-Residents
Non-EU buyers can purchase property in the Canary Islands without restrictions. You will need a Spanish tax identification number (NIE), which our agency can help you obtain. The typical timeline from accepted offer to completion is 6 to 10 weeks, depending on financing.

## Financing
We work with three Spanish banks that offer mortgages to non-resident buyers, typically financing up to 70% of the property value for non-residents and up to 80% for residents. Pre-approval usually takes 5 to 7 business days once documentation is submitted.

## Additional Costs
Buyers should budget for approximately 10 to 13% of the purchase price in additional costs, covering transfer tax (6.5% in the Canary Islands), notary fees, land registry fees, and legal fees.

## Rental Management
For investment properties, we offer full rental management including guest communication, cleaning coordination, and dynamic pricing. Management fees are 18% of gross rental income for short-term holiday lets, or 8% for long-term residential leases.
